1. About this application

This privacy policy applies to ID Design Reference Archive, operated by 深圳市博思纵横设计有限公司.

ID Design Reference Archive is a tool for personal and internal use only. It helps the authorized Pinterest account owner organize and locally archive industrial design references. It is not offered as a public Pinterest service.

2. Pinterest data accessed

The application accesses only data authorized by the Pinterest account owner, within the permissions they grant:

  • Pinterest Boards
  • Pinterest Pins
  • Pin metadata, including titles, descriptions, and related details
  • Image references associated with authorized Pins

3. How data is used

Pinterest data is used only for industrial design research, reference organization, and local archiving. It is not used for advertising or sold as a data product.

4. Storage and retention

Downloaded images and archive records are stored primarily on the user's local computer. Local archive data remains there until the user deletes it. Users control their local files and any backups they create.

5. No sale, rental, or third-party sharing

The application does not sell, rent, or share Pinterest user data with third parties.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

Users can revoke the application's Pinterest authorization at any time through their Pinterest account settings. Users can also delete downloaded images and local archive records from their computer at any time, including any copies in backups they control.

Revoking authorization prevents further authorized access through that authorization; it does not automatically delete files already saved on the user's computer.
